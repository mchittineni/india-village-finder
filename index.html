Village Finder

Every village in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Kerala on an interactive map — with the things a villager actually needs next to it: today's mandi prices, weather, soil & fertilizer guidance, government schemes and land parcels.

67,549 villages across five states · updated 01Sep2026 from LGD

How it works

Three steps, no login, works on any phone browser.

Pick your state

Open one of the five state maps below. You can switch state — or switch the whole UI between 7 languages (English, తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, தமிழ், മലയാളം, हिन्दी, اردو) — from the top bar at any time.

Find your village

Type its name in the search box — English spellings match native-script names too — or drill down district → mandal/taluk → village from the side panel.

e.g. “Kondapalli”, “Vadakara”, “Hosahalli”…

Tap the village

The popup gives one-tap buttons for weather & forecast, soil & fertilizer, mandi prices, government schemes, land parcels with survey numbers, and nearby hospitals, police and offices.

Choose a state

Live counts straight from the latest LGD refresh.

What you get on every village

All from free, open government and community data — refreshed automatically.

🌦️Weather & 7-day forecast Open-Meteo agromet model
🧪Soil & fertilizer profile Soil type, pH, N-P-K guide — ISRIC SoilGrids
🌾Mandi prices, daily Agmarknet via data.gov.in
🏛️Schemes for farmers Central + state — myScheme
📐Land parcels & survey numbers State cadastres + FMB portal links
🏥Nearby services Hospitals, police, offices — OpenStreetMap
🗣️7-language interface Native-script village names included
⬇️Download the data CSV per state, on every release
